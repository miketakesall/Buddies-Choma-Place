Our Story

Where every cut
tells a story of fire

Nestled on the ground floor of Githurai Complex, Buddies Choma Place has been the neighbourhood's open-air secret since 2022. We slow-cook over real charcoal, hand-chop the kachumbari fresh every evening, and stay open until the last group of friends has had their fill.

No shortcuts. Just smoke, heat, and honest meat served the way it was meant to be — with your hands, among buddies.
